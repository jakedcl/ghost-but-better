Ghost But Better

What it does

Ghost But Better is a fork of Ghost. You run it on your Mac or Windows PC, pick a place on a map (or follow a road route), and apply that location to a connected iPhone or Android phone over USB or the same Wi-Fi network.

Everything stays on your machine: saved places, settings, and recovery data. Map tiles and search use public services (OpenStreetMap, Photon, OSRM) only when you use those features.

This fork

  • No sign-in and no Ghost account.
  • No usage telemetry in dev or installed builds.
  • Upstream Ghost is credited; we did not write the original app.

Source and builds

Code, setup guide, and (when published) installers live on GitHub: jakedcl/ghost-but-better.
